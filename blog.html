My Digital Marketing Blog

I started this blog to write about the things I come across while learning digital marketing. Here, I will share my experience with SEO, website creation, content and other online marketing work. I will also post about the small projects I work on and the things I learn while doing them.

Why I Started Learning Digital Marketing

Digital marketing caught my eye because businesses today need more than a product or service. They also need to reach the people online. From search engines and social media, to websites and online content there are ways a business can build its presence. While learning I have started exploring areas of digital marketing and seeing how they work together.

What I Am Learning About SEO

SEO is one of the areas I am working on now. I am trying to understand how search engines look at websites and how good content can make a site easier to find. I am not just throwing keywords into pages. I am learning to write page titles, useful meta descriptions and well-organized content so that both people and search engines can easily understand what a page is, about.

Building Websites

Website building is another part of my learning journey. I also pay attention to how a person can go from one page to another and find the information they need.

Learning Through Projects

Projects have been a part of my digital marketing learning. Of only studying concepts I try to apply them by creating pages writing content working on SEO elements and checking how the website looks on different devices. Working on a project also helps me notice problems that may not be obvious when learning only from theory.

My Next Steps

I am working every day to get better at SEO. I am working every day to get better at content marketing. I am working every day to get better at building websites. I am working every day to get better at parts of marketing. I am focused on gaining experience. I am focused on figuring out how each part of marketing plays a role in supporting a business. I believe the more I understand the better I can help a business succeed. I am excited to keep learning. I am excited to discover ways to make a difference.
